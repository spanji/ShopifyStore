## Evergreen occasions (year-round demand)

Birthday, thank you, congratulations, get well soon, sympathy, new baby, new home, wedding, anniversary, retirement, and just because. Corporate gifting peaks in October to early December (client and staff Christmas gifts) and also follows company milestones. Weddings cluster from May to September; graduations from May to July.

These are the store's core collections. Build them first and give them the most content.

## Demand windows (rules of thumb; check Google Trends for the market)

- Searches for a gifting day usually start rising 4 to 8 weeks before the day and peak in the final 1 to 2 weeks.
- Christmas gift searches build from September to October and peak from late November to mid December. Corporate orders come earlier.
- A page needs time to be crawled, indexed and trusted before demand arrives. That is why the collection-live column sits 8 to 10 weeks out, and earlier for Christmas.
- Last order dates are set by the carrier's cut-offs plus the store's own dispatch days. Publish them on the collection page, in the announcement bar and in emails about 3 to 4 weeks out.

## After each season

1. Take a results snapshot (`performance-report`): sales by box, conversion rate, top search queries, add-on attach rate, sell-through and leftover stock.
2. Switch the collection to its "back next year" state. Keep the URL, intro, FAQs and sign-up form.
3. Note what to change next year in `docs/brand-guide.md` under "Seasonal learnings".
