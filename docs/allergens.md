## Birthday Pamper Hamper for Her

### Summary (the 8 jar sweets, the chew bar and the lollipop)

- **Contains:** **wheat (gluten)** (Fizzy Strawberry Pencils, Halal Fizzy Mix), **soya** (chew bar).
- **May contain:** gluten (Sour Neon Worms, Teeth & Lips, pencils), soya (pencils), and from the Halal Fizzy Mix and lollipop factories: peanuts, tree nuts, milk, egg, sulphites (fizzy mix), wheat, milk, egg, soya, nuts, peanuts (lollipop).
- **Diet:** the jar is not suitable for vegetarians or vegans: 7 of its sweets contain beef gelatine (5 say halal beef gelatine) and 3 contain beeswax. The pencils, chew bar and lollipop have no gelatine. The chew bar box carries Gluten free, Vegetarian & Vegan and Halal icons.
- **Colour warning needed on the jar:** E102 (tartrazine), E110 (sunset yellow) and E129 (allura red AC) "may have an adverse effect on activity and attention in children" (hearts, pencils, fizzy mix, teeth & lips).
