Step 2 · Settings → Policies → Refund policy

We want every box to make someone's day. If something isn't right, please message us first at bowandbloomgifts@outlook.com or on 07597 600465, and we'll sort it out together.

Changed your mind?

You can cancel your order within 14 days of the day it's delivered. Tell us by email or phone, then send the box back within 14 days of telling us. Please talk to us before you post anything, so we can help.

  • Return postage is paid by you, unless we agree otherwise.
  • Items should come back unused and as you received them. We may not be able to take back opened skincare, soaps or sweets for hygiene reasons, but get in touch and we'll look at every case individually.
  • We'll refund you within 14 days of receiving the return, to your original payment method. Standard delivery is free, so there's nothing to refund for it. The £2.99 next-day upgrade isn't refunded on change-of-mind returns.
  • If the box was a gift, the refund goes to the person who paid for it.

Damaged, faulty or not what you ordered?

Message us as soon as you can with a photo, and we'll work out the best fix with you, whether that's a replacement or a refund. This doesn't affect your legal rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

Lost parcels

If your parcel hasn't arrived, let us know. Once Evri confirms it's lost, we'll send a replacement box, or refund you if that box is out of stock.

Step 3 · Settings → Policies → Shipping policy

We deliver to addresses in the United Kingdom, using Evri.

Delivery options

  • Free tracked delivery on every order: usually 2–3 working days after dispatch.
  • Next day: £2.99. Order by 12pm Monday to Saturday and it's usually delivered the next working day. Evri doesn't guarantee next-day delivery, so if it must arrive by a certain date, please order a day or two early.

Dispatch

Orders placed by 12pm Monday to Saturday are sent the same day. Orders after 12pm go out on the next dispatch day, and Sunday orders go out on Monday.

Tracking

We'll email you a tracking link once your box is on its way.

Sending a gift

Just enter their address as the delivery address at checkout, and we'll send the box straight to them.

Something wrong with your delivery?

If your parcel is late, lost or arrives damaged, message us at bowandbloomgifts@outlook.com or on 07597 600465. See our refund policy for how we put things right.
