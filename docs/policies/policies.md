## Refund policy

To cancel, return or tell us about a problem, email bowandbloomgifts@outlook.com or call 07597 600465.

**Changing your mind**
You can cancel within 14 days of the day your order is delivered. Tell us you'd like to cancel, then send the box back within 14 days of telling us. You pay the return postage. We'll refund you within 14 days of receiving the box back.

For hygiene reasons, sealed skincare, soaps and sweets can't be returned once they've been opened.

If the box was a gift, the person who ordered it needs to contact us.

**Damaged, faulty or not as described**
Message us with a photo as soon as you can. Your legal rights apply, which can include a replacement or a refund.
