## Refund policy

To cancel, return or tell us about a problem, email bowandbloomgifts@outlook.com or call 07597 600465.

**Changing your mind**
You can cancel within 14 days of the day your order is delivered. Tell us you'd like to cancel, then send the box back within 14 days of telling us. You pay the return postage. We'll refund you within 14 days of receiving the box back.

For hygiene reasons, sealed skincare, soaps and sweets can't be returned once they've been opened.

If the box was a gift, the person who ordered it needs to contact us.

**Damaged, faulty or not as described**
Message us with a photo as soon as you can. Your legal rights apply, which can include a replacement or a refund.

## Privacy policy

**Who we are**
Bow & Bloom is run by Janna Serry, a sole trader, at 17 Lady Royd Close, Bradford, BD8 0FD, United Kingdom. We're responsible for the personal information you give us. Contact us at bowandbloomgifts@outlook.com or 07597 600465.

**What we collect**
- Your name, email address, phone number, and billing and delivery addresses
- The recipient's name and address, if you send a gift
- Your order, including any gift message
- Messages you send us
- Your email address, if you sign up for our emails
- Basic information about how you use our site, through cookies

We don't see or store your full card details. Payments are handled by our payment providers.

**Why we use it**
- To take, pack and deliver your order (to carry out our contract with you)
- To reply to your messages (our legitimate interest in helping you)
- To send emails you've signed up for, until you unsubscribe (your consent)
- To keep the records tax law requires (a legal obligation)
- To keep the site working and secure and to see how it's used (our legitimate interests, or your consent for optional cookies)

**Who we share it with**
- Shopify, which runs our online shop
- Our payment providers, to take payment
- Evri, to deliver your box

We never sell your information. Shopify may store information outside the UK, using legal safeguards for international transfers.

**How long we keep it**
Order records are kept for as long as tax law requires, usually six years. Email sign-ups are kept until you unsubscribe.

**Cookies**
The site uses the cookies it needs to work, such as for your basket. Other cookies are only used if you agree to them in the cookie banner.

**Your rights**
You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, object to how we use it, or withdraw your consent. Email us to do this. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

**Changes**
This policy may be updated. The latest version is always on this page.

## Shipping policy

We deliver to UK addresses with Evri.

**Delivery options**
- Free tracked delivery on every order, usually 2–5 working days after dispatch.
- Next day, £2.99, usually delivered the next working day after dispatch. Evri doesn't guarantee next-day delivery, so if your box must arrive by a certain date, please order a day or two early.

**When we send your box**
Orders placed by 12pm Monday to Saturday are sent the same day. Later orders go out on the next dispatch day, and Sunday orders go out on Monday.

**Sending a gift**
Enter their address as the delivery address at checkout and we'll send the box straight to them.
