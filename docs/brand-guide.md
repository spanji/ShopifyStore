## Visual system

Filled in by the `storefront-design` process. Record the decision and the reason.

- **Palette** (named hex values and their roles), matched to the logo and banner on 9 Oct 2026: Cream #FBF4EF (page background), Petal #F3E3DC (alternate sections), Rosewood #9C5B5F (primary buttons, white text 5.1:1; links on cream 4.7:1), Deep rosewood #7E4549 (links on petal), Dusty rose #D7A9A6 (delivery band, borders), Cocoa #4A2C2A (text, footer, announcement bar; from the logo ink), Sage #8C9A80 (eucalyptus; tiny accents only), Berry #A8325A (buy buttons only; hover #86244A; white text 6.4:1). Replaces the earlier brighter blush and rose. Berry added 10 Oct 2026 because the owner found the rosewood buttons "too disguised": every other colour on the site is a muted rose or brown, so a buy button in the same family blends in. Berry keeps the rose hue but has about twice the saturation, so it is the one thing that stands out, and it is used for nothing except buying, so people learn that berry means "buy". It is the primary button colour of colour schemes 1 and 4, so it can be changed in Theme settings, Colours.
- **Typefaces and type scale:** Playfair Display for headings (close to the logo's high-contrast serif) with Assistant for body, menus and buttons. Two families only. Scale is the theme's presets.
- **Photography style and product image ratio:** real photos of the actual box only (the launch image is AI-enhanced and must be replaced). Warm daylight, pink tissue and ribbon, open-lid hero shot plus close-ups. Product cards crop square.
- **Spacing, radius and shadow tokens:** theme defaults for now: pill buttons (radius 37), 8px inputs, no card shadows.
- **Seasonal colour schemes:** none yet. Theme colour schemes: scheme-1 cream base, scheme-2 cocoa overlay, scheme-3 cocoa (footer, announcement), scheme-4 petal (hamper feature), scheme-5 dusty rose (delivery band), scheme-6 rosewood (marquee, sale badge). Add Christmas or Valentine's as new schemes rather than editing these.
- **Logo files:** `docs/brand/` holds the banner, the wordmark (header logo, transparent PNG cut from the banner), the BB monogram and the favicon. The same files are in Shopify Files as bow-and-bloom-*. The banner shows flowers, which the store doesn't sell, so the homepage headline directly under it names the product.
- **Theme:** two unpublished versions for the owner to compare (10 Oct 2026), with the same code. "Bow and Bloom v5 (motion)" (theme 197116002637, a copy of v4) has the framed hero: "Hampers for your best friend / girlfriend / sister / mum / daughter" beside the hamper photo in an upright frame, with the photo below shown as a close-up so it doesn't repeat. "Bow and Bloom v6 (banner)" (theme 197122687309, a copy of v5) brings back the v1 banner (owner, 10 Oct 2026: the flower banner across the full width, filling the first screen on desktop) and moves the changing words to a shorter statement section straight after it. The owner then found "Birthday hampers she'll love" under the banner too similar to the statement, so the banner now stands alone and the statement is the page's main heading (h1). Both keep the bow intro, berry buy buttons, homepage motion and the new phone menu; `theme/templates/index.json` is v6 and `theme/variants/v5-index.json` is v5. Earlier: "Bow and Bloom launch" (copy of the Horizon-family Balance theme, created 9 Oct 2026) holds the new homepage, colours, fonts, announcement bar, footer and the free gift message field. "Bow and Bloom v2 (ribbon)" (theme 197109285197) adds the ribbon layer below and has been the live theme since the owner published it on 10 Oct 2026. Edited files are copied in `theme/`.
- **Bow motif** (10 Oct 2026): one satin bow vector, two loops, a knot and two tails, in `theme/snippets/bow.liquid` (filled, or `style: 'line'` to draw itself) and as CSS masks (`--bb-bow`, `--bb-pattern`) in `theme/assets/bow-and-bloom.css`. It is the brand mark everywhere small: list bullets, FAQ group headings, the gift message heading, the chosen occasion, the add-to-basket icon. Reason: the box ships tied with a satin bow, so the bow is the thing customers actually see on opening.
- **Ribbon divider:** a "Ribbon divider" section (`theme/sections/ribbon-divider.liquid`) with ribbons that unroll and a bow that draws itself on scroll. Use it between major sections, not between every one.
- **Pattern:** a faint tiled bow pattern (6–7.5% of the text colour) on dusty rose (scheme-5) sections, the password page and anything with the `bb-pattern` class. Only in empty space, never behind body text on cream.
- **Motion budget** (owner asked for more motion on 10 Oct 2026, replacing "never, or small"): one orchestrated moment, the **bow intro**. On the first homepage visit of a browser session a big bow draws itself, ribbons unroll across like a wrapped present, the bow-print paper parts and the bow flies to its place above the headline (about 2 seconds; any tap, key or scroll skips it; skipped on slow connections and for reduced motion; never in the theme editor). Homepage only, because TikTok visitors land on the product page and shouldn't wait. Looping touches, all subtle and slow: the changing headline word, the turning price badge, floating bows round the hero photo, the swaying divider bow, the drifting bow print on dusty rose bands, the marquee, and a satin sheen across buy buttons every 6 seconds (three times on the product page, then it stops). Scroll reveals on the homepage: headings rise word by word, text comes into focus, gift tags drop in one by one, the hamper photo unwraps upwards and drifts slower than the page, questions slide in. The contents strip never gets a scroll reveal: Horizon copies its text to make the loop, the copy stayed hidden, and the strip ran out and restarted (owner spotted it, 10 Oct 2026); its join is spaced exactly like the gaps between items. The hero's pause button stops every loop on the page and is remembered (WCAG 2.2.2). All of it uses transform, opacity, clip-path or filter, and all of it switches off for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Hero** (`theme/sections/bb-hero.liquid`, 10 Oct 2026) has three layouts. *Full screen* (v6, from v1): the banner across the full width at its own 3:2 shape, the words centred below, or the photo alone ("Show the headline, button and trust points" off, as in v6) when the next section carries the headline. The intro bow lands on the nearest small bow above a headline on screen (on v6 phones, the statement's) and otherwise flies into the header logo and fades (v6 desktop). *Framed* (v5): the headline beside a photo in a rounded frame (landscape, upright or square) with bow-print wrapping paper behind it. *Banner* (v6, the owner's preferred look from v3): the flower banner fills the right of the screen to its edge (phones: the full width above the words), with a still headline. Both carry the berry button with the live price, trust points (stacked on the banner, as in v3), the turning "17 treats • Free UK delivery • Packed by hand" badge (on the seam between words and photo on desktop) and floating bows kept clear of the words and the photo.
- **Changing words** (`snippets/bb-rotator.liquid`): the last words of a line change in rosewood italic with a ribbon underline that draws itself. Google and screen readers read the whole sentence once from hidden text; the moving letters are drawn by CSS so the heading isn't doubled. The old word clears letter by letter and the next starts only once it has fully gone (the owner saw letters jumble and clip in the first version, 10 Oct 2026); every word shares one place so nothing jumps, letter boxes are padded so Safari can't clip italic tails mid-animation, the size shrinks to fit the longest word, and each word stays long enough to arrive and be read. Checked every frame of full loops on phone and desktop: no overlap, no clipping, no movement.
- **Statement** (`theme/sections/bb-statement.liquid`, v6): "Hampers for… birthdays / get-well wishes / congratulations / saying thank you / just because / every moment", centred, with a small bow, "17 pamper treats for whatever you're celebrating. Pick the occasion when you order and add a free gift message." and a "Choose your occasion" link. Owner asked for occasion words (10 Oct 2026); "get well soon" became "get-well wishes" so the sentence reads correctly. It is the page's h1 under the photo-only hero ("Main page heading" setting). Placed straight after the hero and in the product section's petal colour, so it opens the product (the second screen, where attention is high) and stays away from the scrolling contents strip, which moved below the product. It plays as it scrolls into view.
- **Buy button** (`snippets/bb-cta.liquid`, block "Buy button"): berry, 60px tall (64px in the hero on desktop), full width on phones, label, live price after a divider, and a white arrow chip that slides through on hover. A floating copy slides up on phones whenever no other buy button (or the footer) is on screen; on v6 that includes the first screen, since the banner has no button. In the homepage product section it sits under the hamper's name, above the list of what's inside (owner, 10 Oct 2026: it was too far down). Reasons: size (Fitts), isolation (the only saturated colour), the price up front so there are no surprises, and a constant route to buy on long phone scrolls.
- **Header on tablets:** touch screens such as iPads get the phone header (menu button and icons); its icons sit on the page margin, as on desktop, instead of at the screen edge (owner found them too close to the sides, 10 Oct 2026).
- **Phone menu** (10 Oct 2026): serif links with hairlines and an arrow that wakes on touch, the current page in rosewood italic with a bow, then a buy card (photo, name, "Free tracked UK delivery", berry button) and the email and TikTok links. Main menu: Shop, About, Corporate & bulk, FAQ, Contact (owner approved).
- **Questions:** FAQ answers open on tap or click (`<details class="bb-faq">`), not hover, because most visitors come from TikTok on phones, which have no hover. The plus turns into a cross. Source copy in `docs/content/`.
- **Product page emphasis:** the title is balanced and larger, the price uses the heading font in rosewood, and the add-to-basket button is berry, 60px tall, with the bow icon, so it is the strongest thing on the page. The sticky add-to-basket bar matches and keeps the basket icon so its purpose is unambiguous.
- **Hero placement:** superseded on 10 Oct 2026 by the hero above (text and button first on phones, banner beside it on desktop).
