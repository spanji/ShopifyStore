Delivery

How much is delivery?

Tracked UK delivery is free on every order, usually 2–5 working days after dispatch. Next day is £2.99.

When will my box be sent?

Order by 12pm Monday to Saturday and we'll send it the same day. Later orders go out on the next dispatch day, and Sunday orders go out on Monday.

Is next day guaranteed?

Next day orders are usually delivered the next working day, but our courier, Evri, doesn't guarantee it. If it must arrive by a certain date, please order a day or two early.

Can I send it straight to the birthday girl?

Yes. Just enter her address as the delivery address at checkout.

Do you deliver outside the UK?

Not at the moment. We only deliver to UK addresses.

Our boxes

What's inside?

Each product page lists everything in the box. Our Birthday Pamper Hamper for Her has 17 treats, from a face roller set to a jar of sweets.

Will my box look exactly like the photo?

Every box is packed by hand, so colours and designs may vary slightly. If an item runs out, we may swap it for one of similar or higher value and style.

Can I add a gift message?

Yes, for free. Type it in the gift message box on the product page and we'll write it on a card and tuck it in the box.

Do the boxes contain allergens?

Yes. The sweets contain wheat (gluten) and soya, and may contain milk, egg, peanuts, tree nuts and sulphites. The gummies contain beef gelatine, so they aren't suitable for vegetarians or vegans. You'll find every ingredient on the product page, under "Sweets: ingredients and allergens". Questions? Message us before you order.

Is the skincare suitable for sensitive skin?

Skincare and soaps are for external use only. If the person receiving the box has sensitive skin, we recommend a patch test first.

Orders and returns

Can I return my box?

Please message us first and we'll sort it out together. You can cancel within 14 days of delivery.

What if something arrives damaged?

Send us a message with a photo as soon as you can, and we'll put it right.

Can I order lots of boxes for a team or event?

Yes. Tell us what you need on our corporate and bulk orders page.
